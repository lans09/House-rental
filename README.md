# HouseOne 🏠

> **Digital Rental Property Marketplace for Nigeria**

HouseOne is a modern PropTech platform connecting property seekers with verified landlords, property managers, and licensed estate agents across Nigeria. Designed to solve rental fraud, pricing opacity, and informal agency chaos through mandatory verification, transparent cost breakdowns, and in-platform communication.

## 🎯 Key Modules & Features

1. **User Authentication & RBAC**: Role-based access for Seekers, Landlords, Estate Agents, Property Managers, and internal Admins (Moderator, Support, Finance, Super Admin).
2. **Property Search & Discovery**: Real-time filtering by State/LGA, pricing, amenities, and verified-first algorithms.
3. **Property Verification Engine**: Multi-tier document validation (CAC, Title, Authority Letter) + physical/virtual inspection SLAs.
4. **Transparent Fee Breakdown**: Clear itemization of Rent, Caution Fee, Service Charge, Legal Fee, and Agency Fee.
5. **Direct & Masked Communication**: In-app chat with number privacy, inspection appointment scheduler, WhatsApp integration.
6. **Monetization & Gateways**: Tiered agent subscriptions, featured listing boosts, integrated with Paystack & Flutterwave.
7. **Reviews & Fraud Safeguards**: Verified-renter reviews, automated duplicate listing detection (GPS + price matching), 1-click fraud reports.
